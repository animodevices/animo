What we collect

  • Your email (required).
  • Your pet's breed and age, and your city (optional).
  • If you used the Buy buttons on the site: the product and the collar configuration you picked.
  • Whether you asked us to tell you when TYAFF goes on sale.
  • As proof of your consent: the date and time of the application, the form language, the version of this notice and the consent text you agreed to.

We do not ask for your name, phone number or address.

Why

  • To choose participants for testing the TYAFF tracker and to contact you about the testing. Breed, age and city help us build a varied test group and plan device delivery.
  • Only if you tick the separate box: to tell you when TYAFF goes on sale.

We use the data for nothing else and never sell it.

Legal basis

Your consent, under the Law of the Republic of Armenia «On Protection of Personal Data» (Articles 8–10). Giving your data is voluntary, but without an email we cannot consider your application. Breed, age and city are optional.

What we do with the data

We receive, record and store it, use it to contact you, pass it to the services listed below, and delete it.

Until the testing programme ends, and no longer than [[ЗАПОЛНИТЬ: 12]] months after you apply; then we delete it. Your consent is valid for the same period unless you withdraw it earlier.

Your rights

  • Ask what data we hold about you and how we use it, and get a copy.
  • Have it corrected, blocked or deleted, or ask us to stop processing it.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by writing to support@tyaff.com. We then delete your application within 10 working days, tell you within 3 working days after it is deleted, and stop contacting you about testing.

  • You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Armenia (pdpa.am) or to a court.
